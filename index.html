    Why GroShare?

    People who live in shared housing need an easy and reliable way to manage common household supplies (like groceries) and divide the costs. Currently, tracking these shared responsibilities comes with some practical issues:

    • Roommates often rely on group chats or physical notes to track what household items need to be purchased, which easily leads to lost lists or forgotten items.
    • Tracking items manually can be confusing and inconsistent, especially when multiple people contribute to the list.
    • When one person goes shopping, receipts often contain both shared and personal items. Calculating the shared costs manually is tedious and error-prone.
    • Keeping track of who paid for what is difficult. It’s easy to forget who owes money to whom.

    In short: managing shared apartment expenses requires too much manual tracking and calculation across multiple disconnected tools.
